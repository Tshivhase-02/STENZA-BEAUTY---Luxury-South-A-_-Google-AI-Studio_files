## Brand & Style

The design system embodies an editorial, high-end marketplace aesthetic tailored to the South African beauty ecosystem. The visual language bridges warm African hospitality with high-fashion salon elegance. 

- **Personality:** Tactile, refined, reassuring, polished, and effortlessly chic.
- **Target Audience:** Modern South African consumers seeking vetted, reliable treatments (braiding, nails, bespoke skincare, barbering) alongside elite independent beauty professionals building sustainable creative businesses.
- **Emotional Response:** Indulgence, uncompromised trust, calm efficiency, and personal empowerment.
- **Design Movement:** Modern Editorial Luxury. High-contrast typography paired with generous, warm whitespace, subtle diffuse atmospheric glows, and tailored soft-curved cards. Structural clarity prevents clutter while rich visual hierarchy showcases vivid treatment imagery and artistry.

## Colors

The palette balances serene botanicals, rich cosmetic tones, and warm organic foundations:

- **Primary (`#B9A3D6` - Soft Lavender):** Serves as the signature luminous brand identifier. Used for key interactive highlights, active states, premium badges, and soft brand backdrops.
- **Secondary (`#39263F` - Deep Plum):** The anchor for high-contrast moments, high-level structural typography, primary conversion buttons, and dark architectural frames.
- **Tertiary / Accent (`#D9A6B0` - Muted Rose):** Expressive warmth used for curated collections, loyalty accents, and promotional tags.
- **Canvas / Background (`#FAF7F2` - Warm Cream):** Natural, unbleached, skin-friendly foundation that provides subtle warmth without stark digital glare.
- **Surface (`#FFFFFF` - Pure White):** Reserved for elevated cards, modal drawers, and interactive form fields.
- **Text Primary (`#29252D` - Dark Charcoal):** Deep plum-tinted charcoal ensuring WCAG AAA accessibility against white and cream backgrounds.
- **Text Muted (`#77717C` - Soft Grey):** Secondary metadata, sub-labels, and breadcrumbs.
- **Semantic Feedback:**
  - *Success / Confirmed:* `#2D7A54` on `#E8F5EE` (Emerald)
  - *Pending / In-Progress:* `#D9822B` on `#FEF4E8` (Warm Amber)
  - *Critical / Canceled:* `#C0392B` on `#FDF2F2` (Crimson)

## Typography

Typography balances artistic luxury with transactional precision:

- **Headings (Playfair Display):** Conveys editorial elegance and legacy beauty house aesthetics. Used exclusively for marquee branding, category hero anchors, modal titles, and professional profile names.
- **Body & UI (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Geometric, highly legible sans-serif with open apertures and clean curves. Powers all functional service listings, duration metrics, verification metadata, and form controls.
- **South African Currency Formats (ZAR):** Prices must always display the standard "R" prefix without space between the currency code and the figure (e.g., `R180`, `R350`, `R1,200`). Render prices in `Plus Jakarta Sans` bold to guarantee immediate numerical clarity across booking summaries.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout uses a fluid responsive grid structure configured to prioritize visual merchandising and booking friction reduction:

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column fluid grid, max-width 1360px centered. Margin is `3rem`, column gutter is `1.5rem`. Service lists and professional feeds layout in 3 or 4 responsive column spans.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column grid with `2rem` outer margin and `1.25rem` gutters. Reflows cards into balanced 2-column modules.
- **Mobile (Up to 767px):** 4-column grid with `1.25rem` outer margins and `1rem` gutters. Horizontally scrolling card carousels edge-bleed into outer margins using negative margin compensation. Sticky bottom confirmation bars anchor booking actions.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses warm, diffuse tonal elevation rather than dense grey shadows, mirroring diffused vanity lighting:

- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Flat Warm Cream (`#FAF7F2`). No elevation.
- **Level 1 (Cards, Service Lists, Tiles):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) surface framed with a 1px border of `rgba(57, 38, 63, 0.05)` and an ambient drop shadow: `0px 4px 20px -2px rgba(57, 38, 63, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover States, Interactive Cards, Popovers):** Pure White surface lifted with `0px 12px 32px -4px rgba(57, 38, 63, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Booking Navigation, Drawers, Modals):** Pure White with dual ambient elevation: `0px 20px 48px -8px rgba(57, 38, 63, 0.12), 0px 1px 3px rgba(57, 38, 63, 0.04)`.
- **Atmospheric Tonal Glows:** Curated artist spotlights and promotional banners feature soft radial underlays using Primary Soft Lavender at 15% opacity (`rgba(185, 163, 214, 0.15)`) with a 48px blur.

## Shapes

The design system implements balanced, modern rounded geometry (Scale 2):

- **Standard Base (`rounded-base`, 0.5rem / 8px):** Applied to form inputs, search fields, category pills, review boxes, and small verification badges.
- **Card & Surface Container (`rounded-lg`, 1rem / 16px):** Standard curvature for beauty specialist profile cards, treatment summary modules, and media galleries.
- **Sheet, Modal & Featured Showcase (`rounded-xl`, 1.5rem / 24px):** Bottom sheets for time-slot selection on mobile, profile header banners, and primary dialog overlays.
- **Pill (`rounded-full`, 9999px):** Applied to category filters, quick-tag chips (e.g., "Knotless Braids", "Gel X"), verified artisan badges, and primary action buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Deep Plum background (`#39263F`), Pure White text (`#FFFFFF`), `rounded-full` or `rounded-base`, font weight 600. Hover transitions to `#29252D` with subtle Level 2 elevation.
- **Secondary / Accent:** Soft Lavender background (`#B9A3D6`), Deep Plum text (`#39263F`). Hover shifts to a 10% deeper lavender wash.
- **Outline / Ghost:** Transparent surface with a 1.5px border in `#39263F` at 20% opacity. Text `#29252D`. Hover fills with `rgba(185, 163, 214, 0.1)`.

### Chips & Filter Tags
- Height: 36px. Border radius `rounded-full`.
- **Unselected:** Warm Cream or Pure White surface, 1px border in `rgba(57, 38, 63, 0.12)`, text `#77717C`.
- **Selected:** Deep Plum (`#39263F`) background, Pure White text, no border. Accompanied by subtle check or dismiss icons.

### Professional & Service Cards
- Pure White background, `rounded-lg` (16px), Level 1 elevation.
- Image ratio 4:3 for portfolio visual preview with a 12px inner inset radius.
- Contains verified checkmark pill (`#E8F5EE` background, `#2D7A54` icon and label).
- Pricing displayed prominently using `price-md` in Deep Plum, aligned opposite duration tags (e.g., "2h 30m").

### Lists & Appointment Items
- Separated by soft divider lines: 1px solid `rgba(57, 38, 63, 0.06)`.
- Include trailing action chevron, appointment status chip, and explicit ZAR price tags.

### Inputs & Date-Time Pickers
- Height: 48px standard. Background: Pure White.
- Border: 1.5px solid `rgba(57, 38, 63, 0.15)`. Active/Focus state uses Deep Plum border outline with an outer 3px glow in `rgba(185, 163, 214, 0.35)`.
- Placeholder text set in `#77717C` body-md.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size: 20px x 20px. 
- Border: 1.5px solid `#77717C`.
- Checked state fills with Deep Plum (`#39263F`) and a white check icon or centered dot indicator.
